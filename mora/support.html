Common questions

How do I import media?

In the Library tab, import from Photos, Files, or a direct media URL. Mora stores imported media on your device for playback.

How do AI features work?

When you request transcripts, translations, summaries, lookups, or exercises, Mora may securely send the media or text needed for that request to third-party AI providers so results can be generated. Your content is not used to train public AI models.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

  1. Open the iOS Settings app
  2. Tap your Apple ID at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions and select Mora
  4. Choose Cancel Subscription, then confirm

Billing and renewals are handled by Apple. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple billing support.

How do I delete my account?

Open Mora → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and associated server data (subject to limited legal retention). You can also email lens.ai@outlook.com to request deletion.
